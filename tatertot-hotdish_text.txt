This recipe is a hearty, plant-based twist on the classic Midwestern comfort food, designed for a clean, modern web interface. It uses black beans and vegetables to create a savory base, providing a "meaty" texture without the meat.

Hearty Veggie & Black Bean Tater Tot Hotdish
Prep time: 15 mins
Cook time: 40 mins
Yields: 6-8 servings


Ingredients
Tater Tots: 1 bag (32 oz) frozen tater tots
Protein: 1 can (15 oz) black beans, drained and rinsed
Vegetables:
1 cup corn (frozen or canned)
1 cup diced bell peppers (red or green)
1 cup chopped spinach or kale
1/2 cup diced onion
Creamy Base:
1 can (10.5 oz) condensed cream of mushroom soup (or vegetarian substitute)
1/2 cup sour cream (or vegan sour cream)
Seasoning:
1 tsp garlic powder
1 tsp onion powder
1 tsp smoked paprika
Salt and black pepper to taste
Optional: 1 cup shredded cheddar cheese (or dairy-free alternative)


Instructions
Prep: Preheat your oven to 375°F (190°C). Grease a 9x13 inch baking dish.
Mix Base: In a large bowl, combine the black beans, corn, diced bell peppers, spinach, onion, condensed soup, sour cream, and spices. Stir until evenly mixed.
Assemble: Spread the veggie mixture evenly into the bottom of the prepared baking dish.
Top: Arrange the frozen tater tots in a single layer, tightly covering the top of the veggie mixture.
Bake: Bake for 30-40 minutes, or until the filling is bubbly and the tater tots are golden brown and crispy.
Serve: Let it stand for 5 minutes before serving.


Web Design Practice Tips
Ingredients List: Use a checklist format for the ingredients (ideal for user interaction).
Recipe Steps: Use an ordered list with "Steps" styling to make it easy to follow.
Image Styling: Use a high-quality photo of a casserole, focusing on the crispy tater tot topping to evoke comfort.
